Terms of service: 1RaceClub Studio

Last updated: 10 November 2026

1RaceClub Studio ("the app") is an internal tool run by 1RaceClub, a Thai F1 and motorsport channel. It is not a public service. Only the 1RaceClub owner uses it, to manage 1RaceClub's own accounts.

What the app does

  • Publishes 1RaceClub's own videos to 1RaceClub's own YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok accounts, and only after the owner approves each post.
  • Reads those accounts' performance numbers, such as followers, views, likes, comments and shares, so we can see how our content performs.

Who may use it

Only the 1RaceClub owner, signed in to 1RaceClub's own accounts. The app does not let anyone else sign in and does not access other people's accounts.

Platform terms

Using the app also means following each platform's own terms, including the TikTok Terms of Service, YouTube Terms of Service and Meta Terms of Service. Everything we post must follow those platforms' community guidelines.
