Privacy policy: 1RaceClub Studio

Last updated: 10 November 2026

1RaceClub Studio ("the app") is an internal tool run by 1RaceClub for its own YouTube channel. It is used only by the channel owner.

What the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app uses the YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos the owner has approved to the owner's channel (youtube.upload);
  • read the channel's and its videos' public details and statistics: subscribers, views, likes and comments (youtube.readonly);
  • read the channel's YouTube Analytics, such as watch time and average view duration (yt-analytics.readonly).

How the data is used and stored

The data is stored only on the owner's own computer and used only to track how our content performs. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. The sign-in token is stored locally and never published. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and data

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or through Google security settings. To have stored data deleted, email us. We delete it within 30 days.
